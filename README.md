# The Evolution of CNNs and Their Application to Audio Classification

A literature review written as the final project for CSCI 5527 (Deep Learning), University of Minnesota, spring 2025.

Maintained by Jathin Mallampati. Team project with Alex Ojemann and Sarthak Bansal.

**[Read the paper (PDF, 12 pages)](paper/CSCI5527_literature_review.pdf)**

## What it covers

- **Image classification:** how convolutional neural networks developed from LeNet-5 through GPU training, AlexNet, VGG, Inception, and ResNet, and why each change improved results.
- **Audio inputs:** how audio is turned into something a CNN can process, from spectrograms and MFCCs to raw waveforms and learned front ends.
- **Applications:** speech recognition, environmental sound and acoustic scene classification, music classification, and speaker identification.
- **Open problems:** robustness to noise, uncertainty estimates, and interpretability.

The review cites 35 papers.
